            My gut this week

            Gentle ways to help your gut

            • Fermented food daily
              Natto, miso soup, plain yogurt, kimchi or tsukemono. Easy to find at any supermarket in Japan.
            • Fiber at every meal
              Vegetables, seaweed, mushrooms, beans, brown rice, oats, banana, kiwi. Aim for 5+ servings a day.
            • Water
              6–8 glasses a day helps fiber work and keeps the toilet regular.
            • Less sugar
              Sweet drinks and snacks can feed the wrong gut bacteria and may make skin worse for some people.
            • Sleep and walking
              A 15-minute walk after meals and regular sleep help digestion.

            Some research links gut health and skin, but every body is different, so watch your own patterns here. If you have pain, blood in the stool, or toilet problems for weeks, see a doctor (消化器内科).
