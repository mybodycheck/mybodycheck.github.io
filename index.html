Privacy policy

What is saved: your email if you log in, and only what you enter: check-ins, photos, products, weight and settings.

Where: with an account, in a secure Google Firebase database. Without an account, only on your phone.

Why: only to show you your own plan, history and patterns.

Who can see it: only you. Your data is never sold or shared. The app owner can technically reach the database to fix problems, but will not look at or share your data unless the law requires it.

Usage counts: the app counts which days it was opened and when accounts are made or deleted, with no name, email or health data.

Your control: delete everything at any time with the button below.

Health: general wellness tips only. It does not diagnose or treat illness.
